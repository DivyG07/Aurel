## About AUREL
AUREL is a private virtual showroom and auction house for luxury and exotic cars. Visitors can explore real cars in 360°, compare up to three cars side by side, enter brand "worlds", bid in live auctions, sell their own car at auction, join the community forum ("The Paddock"), keep a garage of cars they own, and book private viewings with a concierge. Prices on the site are indicative, in US dollars, and exclude taxes, duties and delivery.

## How things work
- **Sign in / accounts:** Sign in with Google or email and password at https://aurel-showroom-divy.netlify.app/login (protected by a Cloudflare CAPTCHA). New members choose Collector or Dealer and answer four quick questions (account type, top 3 favourite brands, budget and collection size, interests).
- **360° view:** Each car page has Photos and a 360° view. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. Choosing an exterior colour repaints the 360° model where the model supports it.
- **Compare:** Add up to 3 cars with the compare button on any card, then open https://aurel-showroom-divy.netlify.app/compare. The best figure in each row is highlighted.
- **Brand worlds:** Choose a marque from "Marques" in the menu to re-skin the whole site in that brand's colours and style. Leave with the "× world" chip.
- **Spec sheet PDF:** "Download spec sheet (PDF)" on a car page gives an A4 sheet of the exact configuration and price.
- **Auctions:** https://aurel-showroom-divy.netlify.app/auctions. Three kinds of sale: AUREL House sales, Dealers (first-hand / new cars) and Collectors (second-hand / pre-owned). Bids in the final 2 minutes extend the clock (anti-sniping). Some lots have a hidden reserve price. To bid, a member pays a one-time refundable bidder deposit of ₹5,000 (via Razorpay) and becomes a verified bidder.
- **Selling a car:** Any signed-in member can list a car at https://aurel-showroom-divy.netlify.app/auctions/sell with photos, starting bid, optional reserve, auction length (1, 3, 5 or 7 days) and start time. Every listing is reviewed by the AUREL team before going live. Sellers can edit or withdraw until bidding opens and cannot bid on their own car.
- **Reserving a car:** On an available car's page, "Reserve this car" takes a refundable ₹1,00,000 deposit via Razorpay; the car is held and an advisor calls within one business day.
- **Refunds:** Deposits are refundable on request; refunds go back to the original payment method in 5–7 working days.
- **Forum ("The Paddock"):** https://aurel-showroom-divy.netlify.app/forum — posts with categories (Discussion, Comparison, Question, Review, Spotted, News), up to 4 photos, threaded replies with photos, up/down votes, sorting by Hot/New/Top. Members can edit or delete their own posts.
- **My Garage:** https://aurel-showroom-divy.netlify.app/garage — members record cars they own (from the catalogue or any make), with photos, mileage and value, and can show them publicly in Collectors' Garages (https://aurel-showroom-divy.netlify.app/garages).
- **Icons:** https://aurel-showroom-divy.netlify.app/icons — famous people and the cars they're publicly reported to own, each with a source.
- **Concierge / private viewings:** https://aurel-showroom-divy.netlify.app/concierge or "Request private viewing" on any car page. An advisor replies within one business day. Email: concierge@aurel.example.
- **Account:** https://aurel-showroom-divy.netlify.app/account — profile photo, preferences, listings, bids, payments, posts, notifications and account deletion.
